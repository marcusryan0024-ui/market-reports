Week Ahead — Catalyst Map

MONDAY AUG 24 – FRIDAY AUG 28, 2026 · PUBLISHED MONDAY AUG 24
Read this first — the whole week points at Wednesday, and Wednesday is stacked twice. July PCE and the second estimate of Q2 GDP both land Wednesday at 7:30am CT, and then NVIDIA reports Q2 FY2027 after that same close. The Fed's preferred inflation gauge and the largest single-name print of the month inside one session. Jackson Hole then opens Thursday and runs through Saturday, with new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivering his first-ever keynote as Chair on Friday — not a routine reiteration of a known framework, but a first policy framing from a new Chair, landing 48 hours after the inflation print he will be asked about. Monday and Tuesday are the only quiet sessions; treat them as preparation, not opportunity.

MACRO EVENT OF THE WEEK
Jackson Hole — Warsh's First Keynote
THU 8/27 – SAT 8/29 · Warsh speaks FRI 8/28
The Kansas City Fed symposium at Jackson Lake Lodge; 2026 theme is 'Financial Innovation: Implications for Payments and Policy.' This is materially bigger than a normal year because it is Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole as Chair — a first-ever policy framing rather than a restatement of a known one. He is widely expected to have to address inflation directly, two days after the July PCE print. Note the sequencing risk: the market gets the data first and the interpretation second.
THE DATA THAT SETS UP THE SPEECH

Personal income, personal spending, headline and core PCE, plus the second estimate of Q2 GDP, all released together an hour before the open. Core PCE is the number Warsh will be asked about on Friday, so Wednesday's print is what gives his speech its content.

How to Trade the Shape of This Week

Wednesday is not one catalyst, it is two with a gap in between. PCE and GDP at 7:30am CT set the tone for the session; NVDA lands after that same close. A position taken on the morning data is not a position into the earnings print, and treating them as one trade is how a good morning read gets given back at 3:20pm. Decide in advance which of the two you are actually trading.

The NVDA number that moves it is not on the call. The CFO's written commentary posts immediately with the 3:20pm release, and it carries the segment detail and the forward guide. The 4:00pm call is where that gets explained, not where it gets revealed. If the plan is to trade the reaction, the decision window opens at the release, not at the call.

Warsh is an unknown framing, not an unknown number. With a known Chair, Jackson Hole is a reiteration and the market prices the deviation. With a first-ever keynote there is no baseline framing to deviate from, which widens the distribution of outcomes in both directions. That is the textbook case for realising profit before a scheduled event rather than holding through it, and it is why the sequencing matters: the data comes Wednesday, the interpretation comes Friday.

Thursday's semis print into a decided tape. MRVL, and to a lesser degree the rest of the complex, reports the morning after NVDA has already repriced the group. That is a materially different setup from reporting into an open question — the direction is largely set and the print becomes a confirmation or a divergence, both of which are cleaner to read than a coin flip.
