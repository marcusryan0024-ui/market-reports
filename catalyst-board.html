What makes the list

  • A quality event releases a number or a verdict. Deliveries, PDUFA decisions, trial readouts, first-time guidance. A keynote with no figures is on the list because it is dated and tradeable — not because it forces a repricing.
  • Liquidity is the hard filter. Market cap, dollar volume per day and listed expiries sit under every ticker. Red means you cannot size into it, whatever the setup looks like.
  • Every date carries a source. Delivery dates from the verified Apr 2 / Jul 2 pattern; PDUFA from BiopharmaWatch and MarketBeat; conferences from each organiser. A wrong date produces a confident measurement of nothing.
  • Known gaps. Oct 2 is inferred, not announced. FDA advisory committee meetings are unavailable — FDA publishes them only ~30–45 days ahead, so re-check mid-October. Spin-offs and lockup expirations aren't covered. Both trial-readout calendars stop at September, so October–December clinical dates are PDUFA only.
