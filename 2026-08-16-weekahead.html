How to Trade the Shape of This Week

The one mechanical catalyst is Tuesday's open. RDDT index inclusion is forced buying on a fixed date — funds tracking the S&P have to own it, and they have to own it by Tuesday. That is a different animal from an earnings reaction, where direction is unknown until the print. The risk is that it front-ran into Friday's announcement pop, so the setup is about how it handles the actual inclusion, not the news.

Front half is where the clean single-name setups are. Monday is thin. Tuesday and Wednesday give HD, then TGT/LOW/TJX/ADI, with no competing macro until Wednesday at 1:00pm. That is the window where a catalyst trade can breathe. Monday and Wednesday are also 0DTE days.

Read-throughs to have mapped before Tuesday opens. HD → LOW is the obvious one, same-day-plus-one. TGT → WMT is the other. ADI reads across the analog complex. BABA drags BIDU, IQ, ZTO and NTES with it. Mapping these Sunday is worth more than reacting to them Wednesday.

Back half is macro-gated, and the real block is next week. FOMC minutes Wednesday is the only gate inside this week. The heavy sequence is NVDA earnings Wed 8/26 (results 3:20pm CT, call 4:00pm CT) followed immediately by Jackson Hole Aug 27-29. Consistent with the paid-before-events rule, anything working into the 26th is a candidate to close rather than carry through.
