How to Trade the Shape of This Week

Front half is where the clean single-name setups are. Monday is dead — use it to build the list. Tuesday and Wednesday give you HD then TGT/LOW/TJX/ADI with no competing macro until Wednesday afternoon. That's the window where a catalyst trade can actually breathe.

Read-throughs to have mapped before Tuesday. HD → LOW is the obvious one, same-day-plus-one. TGT → WMT is the other. ADI reads across the analog names. BABA drags BIDU, IQ, ZTO and NTES with it.

Back half flips to macro-gated. FOMC minutes Wednesday afternoon, then Jackson Hole opening Friday with Powell Saturday. Consistent with the paid-before-events rule: anything working by Thursday is a candidate to close rather than carry into a weekend where Powell speaks.
