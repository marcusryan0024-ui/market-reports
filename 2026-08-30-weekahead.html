How to Trade the Shape of This Week

Do not read the first ten minutes Tuesday night. Four reports land on one close. PANW carries the most positioning of the four.

Trade Intel off Dell. 19% of Intel's revenue is Dell, and INTC has followed DELL's direction 88% of the time after these reports. A stated hit rate on a name that is not the one reporting.

Buy low expectations, not high ones. MRVL raised both years and fell 8% at +184% YTD. Check what a name has already done before the print, not just what it prints.

Be flat by Thursday's close. Mon-Wed is idiosyncratic, Thu-Fri is macro, and only Friday reprices the curve. An earnings position held into payrolls is a different trade than the one you opened.
